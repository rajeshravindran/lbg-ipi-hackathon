=== PARAGRAPHS ===
[0] Project: "SwiftDrive" GenAI Insurance Agent 
[1] 1. Executive Summary
[2] To replace traditional multi-step forms with a natural language interface. The GenAI Agent will guide the user through the quote-to-bind journey, using conversational context to extract data and provide instant support.
[3] 2. Functional Requirements (GenAI Focused)
[4] | ID | Feature | Requirement Description |
[5] | FR.1 | Intent Recognition | The AI must distinguish between "I want a quote," "I have a question about my excess," and "I need to change my address." |
[6] | FR.2 | Conversational Data Capture | Instead of a form, the AI asks: "Great, what's your car's registration?" It then parses the response to trigger the VRN API lookup. |
[7] | FR.3 | Contextual Cross-Selling | If a user mentions commuting to work, the AI should proactively explain and offer "Business Use" or "Commuting" cover. |
[8] | FR.4 | Real-time Explainability | The AI must be able to answer "What does 'Voluntary Excess' mean?" mid-flow without the user leaving the chat. |
[9] | FR.5 | Sentiment Analysis | If the AI detects frustration or confusion, it must offer a seamless "escalation" to a human agent. |
[10] 3. Updated Technical Architecture
[11]  * LLM Orchestration: An orchestration layer to manage the dialogue state and ensure the AI stays on track (guardrails).
[12]  * Structured Data Parsing: The AI must convert conversational text (e.g., "I've been driving for 5 years without a crash") into structured data points for the rating engine (NCD = 5).
[13]  * Tool Calling and data enrichment: The GenAI must have "tools" to call external APIs (DVLA, CUE, Payment Gateway, Data enrichment from Google etc) directly via function calling within the chat.
[14] 4. Conversational Journey Logic
[15] Step 1: The Hook
[16] > AI: "Hi! I'm your SwiftDrive assistant. To get you a quote in under 2 minutes, what’s your car's registration plate?"
[17] > 
[18] Step 2: Interactive Validation
[19] > AI: "Found it! A 2022 Blue VW Golf. Is that the one? Also, where is it usually parked at night?"
[20] > 
[21] Step 3: Soft-Closing
[22] > AI: "Based on what you told me, I can get you covered for £450 a year. This includes your 5 years of no-claims. Would you like to see what’s included, or are you ready to buy?"
[23] > 
[24] 5. Compliance & Guardrails (The "Safety" Layer)
[25]  * Hallucination Protection: The AI must never promise a price it hasn't verified through the pricing engine.
[26]  * Scripted Disclosures: Critical legal text (like the "Statement of Fact") must be presented as a non-editable, static block of text within the chat to ensure 100% accuracy.
[27]  * Audit Trail: Every chat transcript must be saved and timestamped as part of the customer's policy record.
[28] 
[29] User Story: The Conversational Quote-to-Bind
[30] Title: Conversational Vehicle Onboarding via GenAI Agent
[31] Persona: New Customer (e.g., "Alex")
[32] The Story
[33] > As a prospective insurance customer who finds traditional forms tedious and confusing,
[34] > I want to provide my details through a natural, back-and-forth conversation with an AI agent,
[35] > So that I can get an accurate motor insurance quote and purchase a policy without feeling like I’m filling out a tax return.
[36] > 
[37] Acceptance Criteria (AC)
[38] These define the "Definition of Done" for the developers.
[39] 1. Identity & Vehicle Discovery
[40]  * AC 1.1: The AI must initiate the conversation by asking for the Vehicle Registration Number (VRN).
[41]  * AC 1.2: Upon receiving a VRN, the AI must call the vehicle lookup API and present the vehicle details (Make, Model, Year) back to the user for confirmation.
[42]  * AC 1.3: If the user says "No, that's not my car," the AI must allow for manual correction or a re-entry of the VRN.
[43] 2. Intelligent Data Extraction (NER)
[44]  * AC 2.1: The AI must use Named Entity Recognition (NER) to extract variables from natural speech.
[45]    * Example: If Alex says, "I've lived at 12 High St for three years," the AI extracts Address and Time at Address.
[46]  * AC 2.2: The AI must be able to map "vague" job titles to the internal insurance industry codes (e.g., "I fix laptops" \rightarrow IT Technician).
[47] 3. Real-Time Support & Education
[48]  * AC 3.1: If Alex asks "What is a voluntary excess?", the AI must pause the data collection, provide a clear definition, and then resume the flow.
[49]  * AC 3.2: The AI must explain the price impact of changes in real-time. (e.g., "Increasing your excess to £500 will drop your monthly premium by £4.50.")
[50] 4. The "Handoff" & Compliance
[51]  * AC 4.1: Before payment, the AI must generate a "Review Summary" in a clear, bulleted UI card.
[52]  * AC 4.2: The user must explicitly "Confirm" the Statement of Fact via a checkbox or a clear "I Agree" intent within the chat.
[53]  * AC 4.3: The AI must transition the user to a secure, PCI-compliant payment iframe or component to handle credit card details.
[54] Technical Notes for the Team
[55]  * State Management: The system must save the "Conversation State" every 30 seconds. If Alex closes the browser and returns, the GenAI Agent should say: "Welcome back! I still have your Blue VW Golf saved. Should we pick up where we left off?"
[56]  * Fallback Logic: If the AI fails to understand a specific input (e.g., a foreign license type) three times, it must trigger a "Human Agent" chat request or provide a phone number.

[61] Existing Customer:
[62] 
[63] For an existing policyholder, the GenAI agent shifts from a "Salesman" to a "Personal Concierge." The journey must prioritize recognition, speed, and proactive management.
[64] Instead of asking for a license plate, the AI starts with: "Welcome back, Alex. I see you're covered for your VW Golf. How can I help with your policy today?"
[65] 1. The Existing Customer Journey Map
[66] Entry Point: Authenticated Access
[67]  * Trigger: User logs in or biometrically authenticates via the app.
[68]  * AI Context: The agent immediately loads the "Policy Snapshot" (Renewal date, current premium, active add-ons).
[69] Key Service Pillars
[70] | Scenario | AI Action | Outcome |
[71] |---|---|---|
[72] | Mid-Term Adjustment (MTA) | "I’ve moved house" or "I changed my car." | AI recalculates the premium in the chat and handles the pro-rata payment/refund. |
[73] | Claims Reporting | "I've had a bump." | AI switches to "Empathy Mode," collects photos/location via chat, and triggers the FNOL (First Notice of Loss) workflow. |
[74] | Renewal | "Why has my price gone up?" | AI explains the inflationary factors or offers to adjust the mileage/excess to lower the cost. |
[75] | Document Retrieval | "I need my proof of NCD." | AI generates the PDF and sends it via chat/email instantly. |
[76] 2. Updated User Story: Mid-Term Adjustment (MTA)
[77] Title: Conversational Policy Update
[78] As a policyholder who has just bought a new car,
[79] I want to tell the AI my new registration number,
[80] So that I can update my cover and pay any price difference without calling a contact center.
[81] Acceptance Criteria:
[82]  * AC 1: AI must validate that the user is the primary policyholder.
[83]  * AC 2: AI must calculate the "Difference in Premium" (IPT included) based on the remaining days of the policy.
[84]  * AC 3: AI must inform the user of any administration fees before they commit to the change.
[85]  * AC 4: Upon payment, the AI must instantly replace the old Certificate of Insurance with the new one in the user's digital wallet.
[86] 3. The "Renewal" Happy Path
[87] This is where GenAI prevents "Churn" (customers leaving for competitors).
[88] User: "My renewal is too expensive. Why is it £50 more this year?"
[89] GenAI Agent: "I hear you, Alex. I’ve looked into it—the increase is mainly due to a general rise in repair costs for VW parts. However, I see you estimated 10,000 miles last year but only drove 6,000. If we update your annual mileage to 6,000, I can bring that price down by £35. Would you like me to apply that?"
[90] User: "Yes, please."
[91] GenAI Agent: "Done! Your new renewal price is £415. Shall I set that to auto-renew on the 1st of next month using your card ending in 1234?"
[92] 4. Advanced "Proactive" Features
[93] To make the AI feel truly "Next-Gen," it should anticipate needs:
[94]  * The "Price Drop" Alert: "Hi Alex! I noticed you've now held your license for another year. This qualifies you for a small discount on your remaining months. I’ve applied a £12 credit to your account!"
[95]  * The "Weather Warning": "Hey, there's a heavy hailstorm predicted in your area tonight. If you can, park the Golf in the garage to keep it safe!"
[96]  * The "Claims Assistant": If a claim is open, the AI should provide a progress bar: "Your car is currently at the body shop. They expect to have it ready by Thursday!"
[97] 5. Security Guardrails for Existing Customers
[98]  * Step-up Authentication: For high-sensitivity changes (like changing the bank account for payouts), the AI must trigger an SMS OTP (One-Time Password) mid-chat.
[99]  * Verification of Fact: If a user changes their address to a high-theft area, the AI must explicitly state: "Moving to this area changes your risk profile. Your new monthly cost will be £X."
